OpenBareMap is an offline topographic map for hiking, available as separate regional versions. OpenBareMap is designed to work across iOS, Android and Garmin with minimal changes. Downloaded maps remain usable in areas of poor cellular or Wi-Fi coverage. Designed for hiking, it combines 20-metre contours, hillshade, paths and tracks, land-cover detail and named summits.

The map companion

OpenBareMap

Choose a regional map to preview. Each opens an interactive map with its own coverage, place menu and map key. Appearance on a Garmin unit will differ slightly due to internal Garmin constraints.
