OpenBareMap {{LABEL}} 2.0.0 — Garmin Map
For compatible Garmin mapping products

1. Contents
{{FILENAME}} contains the regional display-only topographic map,
20-metre contours, native elevation data, land-cover styling and place names.
It has its own map family identifier ({{FAMILY}}) and can coexist with other
OpenBareMap regions. It provides no turn-by-turn routing.

2. Requirements
Use a Garmin mapping product that accepts custom .img maps. Check its manual
for custom-map support, storage location and map-management controls. Ensure
there is enough free storage for the complete download. Keep its original map
files and your personal data intact.

3. Installation
Download {{FILENAME}} and verify its SHA-256 against:
https://downloads.appsbyonzy.dev/Garmin/{{SLUG}}-SHA256SUMS.txt
Place the complete .img file in the Garmin map folder on supported internal
storage or removable media, following your product's manual. Retain the supplied
filename. Remove only superseded copies of this same OpenBareMap region; do not
remove factory maps, other regional editions or personal overlays. Safely
disconnect the storage, restart or refresh maps as needed, and enable OpenBareMap
{{LABEL}} through the map settings. Use metres for metric contour labels.

4. Verification and offline use
Confirm the region is listed and enabled. Check an overview and detailed views
of known mountains, coast and settlements. The installed map works offline.
If it is not listed, check the file finished copying, its checksum matches and
the storage location is supported. Avoid enabling duplicate copies of the same
regional edition. Rendering and available controls depend on the Garmin product.

5. Attribution and distribution
OpenStreetMap-derived data is distributed under ODbL 1.0. Preserve the map and
terrain notices when redistributing it:
https://downloads.appsbyonzy.dev/Garmin/{{SLUG}}/ATTRIBUTION.txt
https://downloads.appsbyonzy.dev/Garmin/{{SLUG}}/terrain-attribution.md
https://downloads.appsbyonzy.dev/Garmin/{{SLUG}}/terrain-data-sources.md

6. Compatibility and support
The Garmin rendering uses native fonts, symbols and detail levels. Its appearance
differs from the interactive web and mobile previews. Follow the product manual
for map selection and troubleshooting. This map is a navigation aid; source
coverage and cartographic detail do not establish route suitability or access.
